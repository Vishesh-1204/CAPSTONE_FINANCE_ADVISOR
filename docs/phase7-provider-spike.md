## Follow-up checks (same session, inside the container)

- **Dependency conflict:** `pip install yfinance` pulled `pandas 3.1.1`, which requires `numpy>=2.0.2`;
  `langchain 0.3.7` requires `numpy<2`. The spike container ended up with numpy 2.4.6 (conflict) and was
  recreated afterwards (back to numpy 1.26.4, no yfinance). If yfinance goes into requirements.txt, it
  needs `pandas<3` pinned next to it so numpy stays below 2 (to be verified in 7A-3).
- **yfinance pulls extra packages:** beautifulsoup4, curl_cffi, lxml, peewee, protobuf, websockets,
  platformdirs, pytz, requests (licence: Apache-2.0).
- **Partial bar:** the latest yfinance bar is dated `2026-11-09 00:00+05:30` (Asia/Kolkata) and was
  fetched at 09:56 IST, after the 09:15 open. It is today's unfinished session, not a closed day. The
  adapter must drop any bar dated today (IST) before the 15:30 close, or the cache stores a fake close.
- yfinance's earliest bar for RELIANCE.NS and HDFCBANK.NS is 1996-01-01 (a holiday), so the very old
  history needs a sanity filter. Only the last few years are needed anyway.

Risks to carry into the design and the paper's limitations:

- yfinance is an unofficial route to Yahoo Finance, not a licensed API. It can rate-limit or break without
  notice, and Yahoo's terms allow personal use only. That is why we use cache-first, the seed script,
  stale-cache fallback and CSV upload (spec section 5), and why no price data is committed.
- Google News RSS links are `news.google.com` redirects (115/115), not publisher URLs. Store the headline,
  publisher, date and link only, never article text. The feed is for personal, non-commercial use.
- Prices are unadjusted (`auto_adjust=False` in the spike); decide in 7A-3 whether to use adjusted closes
  for returns (splits and dividends).
